A City of Legends

A City of Legends

One of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in Central Asia, Samarkand prospered from its location on the Silk Road between China and the Mediterranean. At times it has been one of the greatest cities of Central Asia.

Founded around the 8th or 7th century BC, Samarkand has been ruled by a succession of Iranian, Greek, Turkic, Mongolian, and Chinese empires. The city is noted for being an Islamic centre for scholarly study, and in the 14th century it became the capital of the empire of Timur (Tamerlane).
